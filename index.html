Effective Date: October 3, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the NoMoreShorts - Focus & Blocker app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Kelux (collectively, the "Application"). Kelux is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

1. Use of Accessibility API (AccessibilityService)

The Application utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API solely to provide its core functionality of detecting and blocking short-form videos (such as YouTube Shorts).

Specifically, the AccessibilityService API is used to:

  • Detect when short-form video player components are active on your screen.
  • Automatically perform a navigate-back or scroll action to return you to your main feed or previous screen based on your enabled blocking settings.

Data Privacy regarding Accessibility API:

  • The Application DOES NOT collect, record, log, store, or transmit any personal data, text, keystrokes, messages, screen content, audio, or user information obtained via the AccessibilityService.
  • All accessibility processing occurs strictly locally and in real-time on your mobile device.
  • Accessibility features are used exclusively for video blocking functionality as configured by the user and are never used for tracking, monitoring, or marketing purposes.
