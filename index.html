Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the NoMoreShorts - Focus & Blocker app for mobile devices, operated by Kelux (collectively, the "Service Provider").

1. Zero Data Collection Policy

The Application is designed with user privacy as a primary feature.

  • The Application DOES NOT collect, process, transmit, store, or share any personal data, sensitive user information, location data, or device identifiers.
  • The Application DOES NOT track user activity, usage statistics, or browsing behavior.
  • No user accounts, logins, or personal details are required or requested to use the Application.

2. Local On-Device Data Storage

All application features, including your preferences, schedules, timers, and operational logs, are stored strictly and locally on your own mobile device (via local SharedPreferences).

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by the Service Provider or any external servers. Uninstalling the Application or clearing its app data will permanently remove all locally stored preferences.

3. Use of Accessibility API (AccessibilityService)

The Application utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API solely to provide its core functionality of detecting and blocking short-form videos (such as YouTube Shorts).

Specifically, the AccessibilityService API is used to:

  • Detect when short-form video player components are active on your screen.
  • Automatically perform a navigate-back or scroll action to return you to your main feed or previous screen based on your enabled blocking settings.

Data Privacy regarding Accessibility API:

  • The AccessibilityService operates 100% locally on your device in real-time.
  • It DOES NOT collect, record, log, read, store, or transmit any personal information, screen text, messages, keystrokes, or media content.
  • It is strictly used for performing the video blocking action defined by the user.

4. Third-Party Services & Ads

The Application integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob operates independently under Google's Privacy Policy.

The Service Provider (Kelux) does not collect, receive, store, or process any personal data or advertising identifiers from AdMob. Any technical information processed by Google AdMob to serve ads is governed directly by Google's policies:

  • Google Play Services / AdMob Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

Because the Application does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under any age.

6. Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted to external servers, your data privacy is inherently protected against external network breaches or remote server unauthorized access.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
