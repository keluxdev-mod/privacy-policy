Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the NoMoreShorts - Focus & Blocker app for Android devices (the "Application"), operated by Kelux (the "Service Provider").

1. Summary

  • 100% no screen data collected. The Accessibility permission never records, stores or transmits your screen content, messages, keystrokes or personal information.
  • The Service Provider (Kelux) does not run any servers and does not collect, receive or store any personal data from the Application.
  • No user accounts, logins or personal details are required.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings, timers, schedules, activity log and the lists of YouTube channels and Instagram profiles you choose to block are stored only on your own device (in local app storage). The Service Provider never receives this data.

If Android's own backup feature is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your personal Google account. This backup is managed by Android and Google, not by the Service Provider, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

Uninstalling the Application or clearing its app data permanently removes this locally stored data.

3. Use of the Accessibility API (AccessibilityService)

The Application uses Android's AccessibilityService API solely to provide its core feature: detecting the sections you chose to block on YouTube and Instagram and closing them. These are YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and the optional extra features you turn on yourself (for example comments, stories, search, Explore, the home feed, Reels in the feed, or channels and profiles on your block list).

Specifically, the AccessibilityService is used to:

  • Receive events only from the YouTube and Instagram apps (and from the Application itself, to check that the service is running).
  • Check the structure of the YouTube or Instagram screen (for example, the identifier of the Shorts or Reels player view, or which tab is selected) to recognise when a section you chose to block is open.
  • Only if you turn on the channel or profile blocker: read the channel name shown on the YouTube video page, or the username at the top of an Instagram profile, and compare it with the list you typed in the Application. This comparison happens on your device and the name is not kept.
  • Perform a "Back" action, switch to another tab, scroll the feed, or cover that part of the screen to close the section, according to your settings.
  • Only if you turn on "Watch the first Short" or "Watch Reels sent to you": place a layer over the video that stops scrolling to the next one, and pass your taps through to the video.

Data privacy regarding the Accessibility API:

  • All of this happens 100% locally on your device, in real time, in memory.
  • It DOES NOT collect, record, log, store or transmit any personal information, screen text, messages, keystrokes or media content.
  • No data obtained through the Accessibility API is shared with the Service Provider or any third party.
  • You can turn the service off at any time in your device's Accessibility settings.
